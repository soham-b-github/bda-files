Note: Socket Programming with TCP (cont.)

	Duto scenario (concepts):
		1. Mail pathano. Computer A, computer B ke mail pathachhe. Suppose onek kota attachments achhe, queue kore chunks e pathay, via many routers, so that it reaches the
		destination. This is possible only if connection of aligned routers are connected. So connection oriented. So TCP!
		2. Video Streaming. Video continuously flow te thakte hobe. Chunks kore video pathano jabe na. Video streaming is, akta computer e video cholchhe, sheta onnyo
		computer e dakha jachhe. So chunks kore since not possible, it is UDP!
